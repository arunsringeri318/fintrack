How to Use FinTrack v4.1

  1. Dashboard & Alerts: Tap any top summary card to jump straight to that section. Keep an eye on upcoming chit dues or insurance renewals.
  2. Chits & Daily Pigmy: Track active chit schemes and pay installments with 1-tap. Pigmy deposits auto-sync daily from your start date.
  3. Stocks & SIPs: Record holdings across AngelOne, Zerodha, Anand Rathi, and track monthly SIPs for Self & Spouse separately.
  4. FDs & Insurance: Store guaranteed bank fixed deposits, term plans, ULIPs, and liquid bank account balances.
  5. Digital Vault & IDs: Securely copy Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, or Vehicle RCs in 1 tap.
  6. Backups: Use the top right transfer icon to export JSON backups or create point-in-time snapshots in the Snapshot Center.

  1. Tap the Browser Menu (⋮) icon in top right of browser window.
  2. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  3. Confirm by tapping "Install" to place FinTrack on your app screen.

iPhone / iPad Steps (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button () at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and select "Add to Home Screen".
